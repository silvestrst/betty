# Tauri 2 app for Android and iOS

**Shape of the app.** The UI holds no business logic: it renders view structs and sends commands. Everything that affects a balance lives in Rust. The preferred way to build the app is in [guidance/app.md](../guidance/app.md).

U0 to U5 are Phase 4 and end in the first beta, which has no store listing: a direct APK download on Android and a TestFlight public link on iPhone. U6 and U7 are part of Phase 5: U6 comes after Gate 4 and M9, and U7 after M10 (see [phases.md](phases.md), including "Decisions" for what beta means).

| # | Milestone | Delivers | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U0 | Shell | Tauri 2 mobile project, engine embedded, generated bindings, event stream, app icon and name | A debug build on one Android and one iOS device creates a party and lists it |
| U1 | Mobile plugins | Keychain and Keystore `KeyStore` plugin, Bonjour and NSD `Discovery` plugin, opening `betty://` invite links, opening the share sheet, importing preset files, Local Network permission text | An Android phone and an iPhone discover each other and sync on one Wi-Fi; on both, an invite link received in WhatsApp opens Betty when tapped or works when pasted |
| U2 | Core screens | Onboarding (name, fingerprint), create party with settings, invite link with share and copy buttons, join by tapping or pasting, approval with fingerprint check, party home with members and last seen, activities from presets (pick, adjust, save, share), stakes, results, corrections | A party with a cash game and a pool bet runs on four phones |
| U3 | Governance screens | Inbox of things to accept or dispute, chip-count flow with the sum gap, Leave and Depart, proxy count with witness, handover and takeover, equivocation banner, "missing updates" and "not delivered yet" states, hotspot hint when no other phone can be reached | Every diagram sequence except the Everyone agrees chip count can be performed by tapping |
| U4 | Settle-up | Final balances, transfer list with payee and amount, a "pay your own way" message, Mark sent and received, head fingerprint words, dispute window countdown, revision flow | Every phone shows the final balances and each transfer's payee and amount; each transfer can be marked sent and received |
| U5 | Hardening and first beta | Accessibility pass, light and dark themes, English strings externalised for translation, TestFlight description positioned as a game-night ledger, crash-free field test, the first beta as a signed APK and a TestFlight public link, one install page for both | Two people, one on Android and one on iPhone, run a real party with a pool bet and a head-to-head through to settlement, with no manual fixes to the log |
| U6 | Everyone agrees screens | Mode choice when creating a party, entering your own chip count after a `CountRequest`, accepting or disputing a round or rule change, "awaiting acceptance" and "not accepted by" states, majority close, settlement closed by acceptance | The Everyone agrees chip count sequence can be performed by tapping |

**Distribution notes.** Any description of Betty, starting with TestFlight's, says "ledger" and "units", shows no odds, takes no fee and handles no money, which keeps the app out of the real-money gambling category. iOS needs `NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`, the `_betty._udp` entry in `NSBonjourServices`, and the `betty` URL scheme. The "update Betty" screen links to the install page. Store copy for a listing and the age rating wait until Betty has a public listing.
